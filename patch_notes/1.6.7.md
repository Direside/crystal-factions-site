# 1.6.7 Patch Notes

## Core Mechanics

### Running out of cards

As part of our local playtesting we've noticed that players tend to need to play a lot more troopers than I'd like, in combination with this, it's common for players to be running out of cards to play around turns 5-6 unless they happen to get their card draw going. While the suggestion of drawing 2 cards per turn is tempting, it would easily cause the small deck size to be depleted quickly and also make the Search mechanic not very useful. Instead we are just going to increase the number of cards you have access to in two ways:

- Increase starting draw to 7 and keep 4
- Remove the "skip draw on your first turn" rule

The goal is to allow players to have a few more tools to play with, but still have playing cards matter and keep card advantage as a real consideration.

### Infinite Troopers

The previous rules didn't put a cap on troopers at all. Mostly just because it wasn't something that has come up very often. With players getting more familiar with the decks, it has become more common to see players just play down a lot of troopers while saving cards with powerful Deploy effects for when they will provide the most value. For now we are simply going to address this by putting a cap on the number of troopers you have, as suggested by the playtesters.

- Trooper deck size capped at 15

## Outer Rim Raiders

In the more recent playtests we are seeing a drop in the winrate for ORR, especially in 1v1 due to them running out of steam early and not being able to keep up economically. While the change to card draw will likely help with this, I'm also looking at how to bring their Faction Ability up to the power level of the other factions. Comparing "gain 2" to the 3 and 5 of MMC and EF, it's slightly underwhelming, especially because cards are such a limited resource. Reverting it to gain 3 at the base however is hard because it would allow for bombing run on turn 3 with only 2 cards discarded. To try and meet in the middle, I'm going to reduce the trooper count for the upgrade to 3, meaning you can still get it with the help of Drop Troopers on turn 2, but there is some counterplay to it and you need a specific card combo to pull it off.

- Mining Expedition. ~~5+~~ -> 3+ Troopers: Gain 3 crystals instead.

The other point that has come up in testing is around the limits on certain cards, specifically Rallying Call. Initially there was no limit and it was possible for players to stack a lot of troopers in attack and then just play Rallying Call to win so a limit was added. Given the current state of the game, as well as the new trooper limit, I'm removing the restriction for now to see how things go. If there is a combo that lets you win super early with just troopers and Rallying Call, we can look at adding a limit, but in the games I've observed removing the limit would only add 1 or 2 additional boosts and didn't seem unfair at all.

- Rallying Call. ~~(Limit 5 Boosts)~~
