# 1.6.6 Patch Notes

## Core Mechanics

- Changed all Crystal gaining abilities to scale 1x instead of 2x. This is to reduce the reliance on abilities for income and still require players to use the mining lane

## Faction Changes

### Outer Rime Raiders (Red)

Scrap heap was reduced to match all other factions money gain cards. Personnel Carrier was reverted to 1 troop, but any lane now instead to allow you to bump multiple lanes in the turn.

#### Changes

- Scrap Heap. Deploy: You may Discard a card, if you do draw a card. Aftermath: Gain ~~2~~ -> 1 crystal for each card in your discard pile.
- Personnel Carrier. Aftermath choose one: Draw 2 cards, or add a Trooper to any lane.

### Cybernetic Collective (Blue)

With the goal of simplifying the text on the cards, Strike Drone has been switched to a 2 part ability allowing the disable of a 3 or less normally, but then if you boost the lane enough it can hit a 5. The goal here is to simplify the mechanics and remove the reliance on Drone Swarm from the card.

#### Changes

- Collector Drone. Aftermath: Gain 1 crystal for each boost on this lane.
- Strike Drone. Deploy: For each opponent, DISABLE a card with power 3 or less in any lane. 3+ boosts on this lane: Disable a card with power 5 or less instead.

### MMC (Green)

MMC has retired the Mining Technicians, and along with them the return to hand mechanic. While it was a fun tool to play with, in the hands of newer players it was rarely used, and in the hands of good players it was too much of a swing in momentum to see 5 power disappear from a lane permanently. 

The new card, Tectonic Disruption, works to help the long game of allowing some damage for money and acts as a mini Seismic which helps solidify the theme and potentially open up more aggressive playstyles, instead of waiting for the 2 big cards.

#### Changes

- Third Party Research. Aftermath: Pay ~~2~~ -> **3** to draw 2 cards.
- **NEW** Tectonic Disruption. 7/3, 4/3/3. Aftermath: You may pay 3 CTL to deal 2 damage to your opponents.

### Endracor Federation (Purple)

The big change here is to remove the ability to disable Seismic Manipulation. It's now 9 or less, which hits all the other cards in the game, but it just felt like too much of an easy counter to the one thing MMC could do against late-game Endracor.

Secondly I'm trying out the suggestion of an ultra flexible Disable on Shock Troopers. Allowing you to pick any combination of cards whos power adds up to 3 or less. This simplifies the card text while also allowing them to hit better targets.

#### Changes

- Shock Troopers. Deploy: For each opponent, you may Disable cards with total 3 power or less in their corresponding lane.
